01 — Practice

Training work that holds up under review.

We staff and direct experts who produce the tasks, rankings, rationales, and curricula research teams use to shape model behavior.

  1. Expert model training

    Specialists who write the demonstrations, preference judgments, and corrections frontier models learn from.

  2. Evaluation and alignment

    Rubrics, red-team review, and human feedback programs — including supervised fine-tuning and RLHF — tied to the behavior you want the model to keep.

  3. Multimodal data craft

    Text, image, audio, and video annotation and review, held to a quality bar a research lead can defend.

  4. Domain task design

    Role-faithful assignments with reference material and a gold deliverable — the work a specialist would actually ship.

  5. Instruction for teams

    Workshops in machine learning, generative AI, and analytics that land inside the tools a company already uses.

  6. Platform-ready contributors

    Consultants who already partner with the model-training companies labs rely on, and who know how those programs are run.

Engagements

Three ways teams bring us in.

Post-training

A preference-data squad

A lab needs judgments in a specific domain. We match reviewers, write the rubric, and run a second pass before the signal reaches the model.

Agents

Tasks that mirror a job

Experts design multi-step assignments, the files a professional would consult, and the finished deliverable the model is scored against.

Enterprise

Training that changes the work

Instructors design generative-AI and analytics programs around a team’s real tools, then stay available while the practice takes hold.

02 — Method

A short path from brief to usable signal.

  1. 01

    Brief

    We learn the capability, the failure you care about, and the rubric that defines a good answer.

  2. 02

    Match

    We assign specialists by domain, language, and training method — evaluation, fine-tuning, or task design.

  3. 03

    Signal

    Experts produce tasks, labels, rankings, and written rationales a trainer can inspect.

  4. 04

    Review

    A second reader checks consistency, instruction-following, and rubric fit before delivery.

  5. 05

    Iterate

    As model behavior shifts, we tighten the tasks and the grading notes with you.
